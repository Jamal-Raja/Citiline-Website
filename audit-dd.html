Verify the Numbers. Expose the Risks. Protect Your Deal.

In mergers, acquisitions, or investments, the real value lies in what you can’t see at first glance. Due diligence ensures you know exactly what you’re buying into — by verifying accuracy, identifying risks, and confirming the strength of financial performance before you commit.

At Citiline Accountants, we provide independent financial due diligence that gives you the confidence to negotiate better, avoid costly surprises, and complete deals with clarity.

What We Review

Our reviews go deeper than the headline numbers. We focus on the areas that make or break a transaction:

  • Financial accuracy: verifying reported revenues, profits, and assets
  • Debt & liabilities: identifying obligations and hidden exposures
  • Cash flow sustainability: ensuring the business can support operations and growth

Our Approach

Every engagement is tailored to the deal. We scope quickly, perform targeted fieldwork, and deliver a clear, board-ready report that separates genuine risks from minor issues — with practical recommendations you can act on immediately.

Why Choose Citiline

With over 30 years’ experience, including insights from inside HMRC, we combine technical expertise with commercial judgment. Our role isn’t just to highlight issues — it’s to help you make stronger decisions with confidence.
