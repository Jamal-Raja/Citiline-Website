Making Sense of Global Tax Rules

International operations introduce layered tax challenges — different corporate tax rates, conflicting VAT/GST systems, and evolving rules. Without coordinated planning, businesses risk double taxation, unexpected liabilities, and missed reliefs.

Core Areas We Cover

  • Efficient Cross‑Border Structures: Selecting entity types and locations for subsidiaries/branches that balance tax efficiency and operational needs.
  • Avoiding Double Taxation: Applying the UK’s treaty network to prevent the same income being taxed twice.
  • Transfer Pricing Compliance: Setting arm’s length prices and preparing defensible documentation for connected‑party transactions.
  • Permanent Establishment Risk: Reviewing contracts, staffing, and activities to avoid creating unintended taxable presences.
  • Withholding Taxes: Structuring payments so treaty benefits reduce or eliminate tax at source on dividends, royalties, and services.

Why Coordination Matters

Decisions in one country affect outcomes in another. Where IP is held, how supply chains are routed, and how financing is arranged all carry cross‑border tax consequences. Coordinated planning aligns compliance with cashflow and optimises reliefs across jurisdictions.

Our Approach

We pair UK technical expertise with a trusted global adviser network to deliver clear, compliant solutions for multi‑jurisdiction businesses. We align structures with commercial goals so you can operate internationally with confidence.
