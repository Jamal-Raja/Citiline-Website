What Are Special Purpose Audits?

Special Purpose Audits go beyond statutory requirements. They focus on a defined scope, such as reviewing specific transactions, systems, or financial information. The aim is to provide assurance in areas that matter most to decision-makers.

These audits are particularly valuable when you need independent confirmation for:

  • Lenders and Investors: providing comfort that your financial position supports borrowing or investment
  • Regulators and Authorities: ensuring compliance with industry-specific or government reporting obligations
  • Shareholders and Stakeholders: delivering transparent insight into how resources are being used
  • Management Teams: strengthening decision-making with objective, data-driven analysis

Areas We Cover

We tailor each engagement to your needs, with examples including:

  • Grant & Subsidy Audits: Independent verification of expenditure to secure and maintain government or funding body support
  • Contractual Audits: Assurance over compliance with key supplier, customer, or joint venture agreements
  • Turnover & Royalty Audits: Confirmation of sales figures, royalties, or licensing fees owed under commercial arrangements
  • Compliance Audits: Focused reviews to satisfy sector-specific regulatory or reporting requirements
  • Transaction Audits: Assurance over acquisitions, disposals, restructures, or refinancing events
  • Agreed-Upon Procedures: Bespoke assurance projects designed around your unique requirements
