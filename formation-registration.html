Choosing the Right Structure

Every business has different needs at different stages of growth. We’ll help you choose and set up the structure that best supports your goals:

  • Private Limited Company (Ltd): the most common choice, limiting personal liability while offering flexibility and tax efficiency.
  • Limited Liability Partnership (LLP): ideal for professional practices or joint ventures where partners want flexibility and protection.
  • Sole Trader to Limited Conversion: moving from self-employed to limited company status to unlock tax planning opportunities and greater credibility.
  • Holding & Group Companies: structures that protect assets, separate risks, and support expansion into multiple businesses.
