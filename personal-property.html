Making Property Work Smarter

Property can be one of the most rewarding investments — but without the right tax planning, it can also become one of the most expensive. The difference lies in how well you understand the rules and use them to your advantage. At Citiline, we bring clarity and strategy to property taxation, helping you protect profits and grow with confidence.

Opportunities Hidden in the Rules

Property tax is often seen as a burden, but within the rules are opportunities that can transform how much of your returns you keep. We work with investors, landlords, and developers to identify those opportunities and put them to work. This includes:

  • Holding structures: assessing whether properties should sit in personal ownership, partnerships, or company structures for maximum efficiency.
  • Income extraction: ensuring rental profits are managed in a way that balances short-term cash flow with long-term tax efficiency.
  • Transactions and disposals: planning sales and transfers to minimise Capital Gains Tax exposure.
  • Acquisitions: structuring purchases to avoid unnecessary Stamp Duty Land Tax costs.
  • Development projects: advising on VAT, financing, and reliefs specific to refurbishment and construction.
  • Succession: designing inheritance and trust strategies to pass on property wealth without erosion.

Strategic Guidance, Not Just Compliance

Many accountants focus only on reporting what has already happened. We focus on shaping what happens next. That means looking ahead, modelling different approaches, and showing you the impact of decisions before you commit. Whether you’re growing a portfolio, planning an exit, or restructuring existing holdings, the right advice can save substantial amounts over time.

A Partner Who Understands Property

We don’t believe in one-size-fits-all solutions. Every investor’s goals, financing, and risk appetite are different. Our role is to align your tax strategy with your wider property objectives — whether that’s maximising cash flow, building long-term value, or preparing for succession. The result is a plan that feels tailored, defensible, and commercially sound.
