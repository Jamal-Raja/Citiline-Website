The Right Structure, The Right Start

The structure you choose for your business is more than a legal formality — it shapes how you pay tax, how profits are distributed, the level of personal risk you take on, and even how investors and customers view you. Get it right, and you’ll have a foundation that supports growth. Get it wrong, and you could face unnecessary tax bills, exposure to liability, or difficulties raising finance.

At Citiline, we help you cut through the complexity. Our advice is practical, tailored, and grounded in both tax efficiency and long-term planning. Whether you’re just starting out or restructuring an existing venture, we’ll guide you to the setup that makes the most sense for your goals.

Business Structures We Advise On

Each structure comes with advantages and trade-offs. We’ll walk you through the options and explain them in plain English so you can make an informed choice:

  • Private Limited Company (Ltd): the most common choice, offering limited liability, tax flexibility, and greater credibility with banks and investors.
  • Limited Liability Partnership (LLP): suited for professional practices or joint ventures, combining partnership flexibility with protection from personal liability.
  • Sole Trader to Limited Conversion: moving to a limited structure to reduce personal risk, improve tax efficiency, and enhance your business image.
  • Group & Holding Companies: advanced structures that separate risk, protect assets, and allow profits to be managed more efficiently across multiple entities.

What We Look At

Our advice goes beyond a one-size-fits-all answer. We take into account:

  • Your growth ambitions and funding needs
  • How much personal risk you’re comfortable with
  • Tax planning opportunities and obligations
  • Shareholding arrangements and profit distribution
  • Future plans such as expansion, succession, or exit

Why This Matters

The wrong structure can limit your growth, leave you exposed, or cost more in tax than necessary. The right one gives you protection, flexibility, and confidence in your future. By taking the time to set things up correctly now, you avoid expensive restructuring later and position your business for long-term success.
