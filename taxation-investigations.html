When HMRC Knocks at Your Door

An HMRC enquiry can feel overwhelming. Letters arrive unexpectedly, deadlines are strict, and the risks — financial, reputational, and personal — are serious. Without the right guidance, what begins as a routine check can quickly escalate into a full investigation.

At Citiline Accountants, this is where we excel. With over 30 years’ experience, including time spent inside HMRC, we understand the process from both sides. That insight allows us to cut through complexity, manage the pressure, and position your case for the best possible outcome.

Why Businesses Trust Citiline

Most firms provide general tax compliance. Few specialise in investigations. For us, this is a core discipline. Clients choose us because we combine deep technical knowledge with insider perspective. We don’t just respond to HMRC; we anticipate their approach, challenge their assumptions, and negotiate firmly on your behalf.

Types of HMRC Investigations We Handle

  • Compliance Checks: Routine reviews of tax returns and records.
  • Aspect Enquiries: Focused questions on specific tax areas.
  • Full Investigations: Wide-ranging reviews of business or personal tax affairs.
  • Voluntary Disclosures: Correcting errors and minimising penalties.
  • Code of Practice 9 (COP9): High-stakes cases involving suspected fraud.
  • Negotiations & Settlements: Securing fair outcomes and closure.

Taking Control of the Process

Every case is unique, but our method is consistent. We begin with a clear assessment of the risks, then build a tailored strategy that balances compliance with a strong defence. From there, we take over communication with HMRC and work towards resolution — aiming for swift closure with minimal disruption to your life or business.

Case Study: £472,000 Saved in a VAT Dispute

A client faced an HMRC investigation into the ratio of VATable and non-VATable goods sold in their business. HMRC’s assessment produced a liability of £700,000, a sum that would have placed the company under severe financial strain.

We carefully examined HMRC’s calculations, identified flaws in their approach, and presented a strong technical defence. After detailed negotiations, we succeeded in reducing the liability by £472,000, bringing the final settlement down to just over £228,000.

This result highlights the difference that specialist representation can make in the outcome of a tax investigation.
