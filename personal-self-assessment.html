Stress-Free Self-Assessments Filed Correctly and On Time

Completing a Self-Assessment tax return is a legal requirement for individuals with untaxed income — whether you’re self-employed, a landlord, a company director, or earning additional income. The process can be complex, and mistakes or missed deadlines often result in penalties and unnecessary stress.

At Citiline, we make the process simple. We prepare, review, and file your return with HMRC, ensuring everything is accurate, compliant, and submitted on time. Our aim is to remove the hassle while also helping you keep your tax bill as efficient as possible.

What You Need to Know

Self-Assessment isn’t just about declaring income — it’s also an opportunity to make sure you’re claiming what you’re entitled to and planning ahead for future liabilities. Key considerations include:

  • Deadlines matter: late or incorrect submissions can lead to penalties and interest charges.
  • Different income sources: self-employment, property, dividends, and capital gains all need to be reported correctly.
  • Allowable expenses and reliefs: certain costs, contributions, and allowances can reduce your tax liability if claimed properly.
  • Payments on account: some taxpayers are required to make advance payments towards the following year’s tax bill.

How We Help

That means checking for available reliefs, ensuring expenses are claimed correctly, and giving you clarity on what you owe and when it’s due.

We also deal directly with HMRC on your behalf, saving you time and giving you peace of mind that your tax affairs are in safe hands.
