Specialist Support for CIS Contractors and Subcontractors

The Construction Industry Scheme (CIS) sets strict rules on how contractors pay subcontractors and how tax is deducted at source. It’s designed to bring compliance to the sector — but for many businesses, it creates unnecessary admin, cash flow challenges, and risk of penalties if anything is missed.

At Citiline, we simplify CIS. Whether you are a contractor making deductions or a subcontractor trying to reclaim overpaid tax, we take care of registration, reporting, and compliance so you can focus on running your projects.

How We Help Contractors

Contractors have a duty to register with HMRC, verify subcontractors, and deduct tax at the correct rate before paying them. We make sure your systems are watertight by handling:

  • HMRC registration and verification of subcontractors.
  • Correct application of CIS deductions (20% or 30%).
  • Monthly CIS returns filed on time to avoid penalties.
  • Record-keeping and statements for subcontractors.
  • Advice on managing cash flow while operating under CIS rules.

How We Help Subcontractors

Subcontractors often face deductions at source, reducing take-home pay and creating refund claims at year end. We help by:

  • Registering you with HMRC to avoid higher 30% deduction rates.
  • Applying for gross payment status where appropriate, so you receive full payments without deductions.
  • Preparing and filing tax returns to reclaim overpaid CIS tax quickly.
  • Advising on record-keeping to make claims accurate and efficient.

Keeping You Compliant and Efficient

HMRC penalties for late CIS returns or incorrect deductions can be severe. Our role is to take the pressure off — ensuring everything is correct, submitted on time, and optimised for your business. By outsourcing CIS compliance to us, you reduce admin, protect cash flow, and avoid costly mistakes.

We understand the unique pressures of the construction industry — tight margins, strict deadlines, and constant movement of contractors and subcontractors. Our CIS service is built to give you peace of mind, keep you compliant, and help you get the most from the scheme.
