Protect What Matters Most

You’ve worked hard to build your wealth — your home, your savings, your business, your investments. But without careful inheritance tax planning, a significant part of what you’ve built could be lost to tax instead of passed on to the people you love. Our role is to help you protect that legacy, so your family benefits fully from your life’s work.

Inheritance Tax doesn’t have to be a burden. With the right planning, there are clear, legal ways to reduce your liability and make sure your wealth is transferred exactly as you intend.

How We Help

Every family is different, which is why we take time to understand your personal circumstances, your wishes, and the assets that make up your estate. We then guide you through the rules and reliefs that can be used to protect your estate, including:

  • Nil-rate bands: making full use of tax-free thresholds and transferable allowances between spouses or civil partners.
  • Spousal exemption: ensuring wealth passes efficiently and tax-free between partners.
  • Lifetime gifts: using the “seven-year rule” and annual exemptions to gradually reduce the value of your estate.
  • Business Relief: protecting qualifying business assets from inheritance tax.
  • Agricultural Relief: ensuring farmland and agricultural property qualify for valuable reliefs.
  • Trusts: setting aside assets in a controlled way to provide for future generations while reducing exposure to tax.
  • Charitable giving: passing on wealth to causes you care about, while also reducing the tax burden on your estate.

Our Approach

We know inheritance planning can feel like a difficult subject to raise. That’s why we focus on making the process clear, straightforward, and centred on what matters most to you — your family. We explain the technical rules in plain English, show you the options available, and help you put a plan in place that balances fairness, efficiency, and peace of mind.

Importantly, we don’t just create a plan and leave it there. We review your arrangements regularly so they stay effective as your wealth grows and as HMRC rules change.
